## Where to go from here?

What should you do next, after PUH?

## Haskell

### History
- Simon Peyton Jones, the creator of Haskell, telling the Haskell story (great
talker, and an interesting topic): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re96UgMk6GQ
- A paper going through the history of Haskell (very well written,
light-hearted but also technical):
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/history.pdf 
- Some more history from one of Haskell's creators (why they came up with
Haskell, how they named it, etc.): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LnX3B9oaKzw

### What to learn next
- Typeclasses (writing your own)
- Functor (using them).
- Monad (using them): Maybe, Either, IO, State.
- Applicative, Monoid: useful but you can survive without it.
- Writing tests
  - Check `hspec` on Hackage, QuickCheck
- Monad transformers
  - Check Matija's blog post: http://matija.me/2020/11/05/haskell-monad-transformers-intro/
  - transformers and `mtl` packages on Hackage
- Extensions: don't be afraid! A lot of them are pretty standard (e.g., `LambdaCase`)
