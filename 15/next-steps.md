### What to learn next
- Typeclasses (writing your own)
- Functor (using them).
- Monad (using them): Maybe, Either, IO, State.
- Applicative, Monoid: useful but you can survive without it.
- Writing tests
  - Check hspec on Hackage, QuickCheck
- Monad transformers
  - Check Matija's blog post: http://matija.me/2020/11/05/haskell-monad-transformers-intro/
  - transformers and mtl (mtl!) packages on Hackage
- Extensions: don't be afraid! A lot of them are pretty standard (e.g., `LambdaCase`)
